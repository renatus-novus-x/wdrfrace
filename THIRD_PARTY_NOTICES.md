# Third-party notices

## ndp-x68k and NDP

Wire Drift Racers uses the `ndp-x68k` compatibility player for sound playback.
The player is an unofficial implementation based on the public NDP specification.

- NDP author: naruto2413 (Kazuhiko Naruse)
- NDP website: https://ndp.squares.net/web/
- ndp-x68k: https://github.com/renatus-novus-x/ndp-x68k

No NDP DemoSongs content is distributed in the public build.
